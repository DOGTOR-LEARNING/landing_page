# Dogtor

> Dogtor is a free AI study app for students preparing for AP exams, the SAT and the GRE. Available on the App Store and Google Play.

## What it is
Dogtor (pronounced like "doctor") is an education app whose mascot is a study-whiz corgi. It is not related to veterinarians, animal doctors or pet care.

Students clear bite-sized practice levels on subject "islands". Every question maps to one concept; a wrong answer is explained immediately and saved to a mistake book, and AI chooses the next questions from the concepts the student is weakest on.

## Subjects
- AP Biology
- AP Psychology
- AP Environmental Science
- AP U.S. Government and Politics
- SAT Reading and Writing
- GRE Vocabulary
- Custom: students photograph or upload their own notes, handouts or PDFs and AI turns them into a practice level, for any subject.

Dogtor is not affiliated with the College Board (AP, SAT) or ETS (GRE).

## Features
- AI tutor ("Ask Dogtor"): explains any typed or photographed question, writes practice questions on request, and summarizes recent progress.
- Mistake book for re-practicing missed questions.
- Real-time quiz battles with friends and ranked matchmaking with monthly leaderboards.
- Daily streaks and achievements.
- Dogtor Pro (optional in-app subscription): unlimited hearts, more AI tutor questions a day, more custom levels and no ads.
